## Description

```
30-second demo of the Moon-Material Simulator, our project for the NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026, challenge "Abandoned but not Forgotten".

The machines NASA left on the Moon show what that world does to materials. Our free browser app predicts how NASA Glenn's bacteria-grown bioplastic, mixed with Moon dust, will perform. Rebuild a broken part, test it in 3D, and see the lab evidence behind every number.

▶ Try it (free, in any browser, no sign-up): https://md8-habibullah.github.io/moon-material-simulator/
🎬 The full 4-minute story: https://youtu.be/Bub3y_H-RUI
💻 Source code (MIT): https://github.com/md8-habibullah/moon-material-simulator

IN NUMBERS
• 8 NASA machines on the Moon and Mars, each with its story and the materials lesson it taught
• 1,470 lab values from NASA reports and peer-reviewed papers, every one re-checked against its PDF: 0 transcription errors
• 8 material properties predicted instantly, each linked to the paper and table behind it
• About 5,000 mixes tested by the optimizer, most locally sourced first

Built for engineers, and just as much for students, teachers and anyone who loves rockets and space.

TEAM DREAMS OF X
Mahdin Islam Mukim · Maliha Sanjana · Lamisa Yeasmin Nakia · Yousuf Abdullah · Md. Habibullah Sharif
Five friends, one team: we researched, designed, built and tested it together.

CREDITS
Narration: AI-generated voice (ElevenLabs), reading our own script. All app footage is the real simulator. Photos: NASA (Surveyor 3 and Apollo 12, Apollo 15, Apollo 17). Moon map: NASA SVS CGI Moon Kit. Mars: NASA 3D Resources. Music made with our own code. Sound effects: Pixabay. This project is not endorsed by NASA.

#NASASpaceApps #SpaceApps2026 #AbandonedButNotForgotten
```
