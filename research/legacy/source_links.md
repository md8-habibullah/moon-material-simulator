# Research Papers and Dataset Sources

This document lists the research publications and datasets used for the Moon-Material Simulator project.

**Note:** These links point to original publications, repositories, or abstracts. They are not all downloadable structured datasets. Values must be verified against their original sources before inclusion in the ML dataset.

## Data Collection Policy

- Record the original publication URL for every observation.

- Preserve page, table, and figure references.

- Record units and test types.

- Separate experimental and simulation data.

- Mark chart-derived values as digitized estimates.

- Manually verify extracted values against original publications.

- Do not treat abstracts, secondary reports, or unverified charts as confirmed numerical observations.
