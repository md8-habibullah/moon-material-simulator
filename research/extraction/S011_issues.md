# S011 source issues

Source: Christy (NASA GRC), "Biosynthesized Thermoplastic/Regolith Composites for Closed-Loop In-Space Manufacturing", ACS Fall 2026 slides (NTRS 20260007758). All charts are raster images, so none of the chart numbers are in the PDF text layer.

- p.17 Slide 17 | undefined label | "M-PHB" | The second neat-polymer bar (about 24.3 MPa, vs 29.4 MPa for "Neat PHB") is not defined anywhere in the deck. It could be a different PHB grade, supplier or processing route, so it can't be tied to a specific material.
- p.17 Slide 17 | contradicts companion deck | 40 wt% error bars ±3.21 (JEZ-1) / ±1.75 (LHS-1) / ±4.30 (LMS-1) / ±4.14 (LSP-2) / ±2.42 (MGS-1) MPa vs S006 slide 20, same means, error bars ±2.88 / ±3.20 / ±1.74 / ±4.32 / ±4.15 | The means are identical but the error bars differ. The S006 set looks shifted by one sample. Of the two decks, this one is internally consistent.
- p.17 Slide 17 | missing metadata | "Average Tensile Strength (MPa)" | Number of specimens, error-bar type, test standard and test temperature are not given. The label is printed with a space as "40 % LHS-1".
- p.9 Slide 9 | inconsistent data point | LHS-1 marker at 80 wt% on "Decomposition Temperature" (≈276.0 °C) vs no such point on p.35 "Slow Cooled Samples" (or on S006 slide 16) | All other slide-9 points match the slow-cooled backup chart, but this one is absent there. Its value equals the quenched LHS-1 80 wt% point on p.35 (≈276.0 °C), so it may come from the quenched dataset.
- p.9 Slide 9 | missing data point | "LMS-1" legend entry vs no LMS-1 marker at "80" on "Bulk Crystallinity Values" | No LMS-1 80 wt% crystallinity marker is visible, and it cannot be hidden by the other markers. It is also absent on p.35.
- p.9 Slide 9 | ambiguous sample condition | "Bulk Crystallinity Values" / "Decomposition Temperature" (no cooling condition stated) vs p.35 "Slow Cooled Samples" | The main chart does not say these are slow-cooled films. The match only shows up in the backup charts.
- p.8 Slide 8 | formula / normalisation unclear | "X_C = ∆H_m / ∆H_100% × 100" vs 80 wt% crystallinity of ~26–30 % (p.9) | The formula has no polymer weight-fraction term, yet the values above 20 % at 80 wt% filler mean normalisation must have been applied. ∆H_100% is not given.
- p.35 Slide 35 (Quenched Samples, Decomposition Temperature) | missing data point | "Quenched LHS-1" legend entry vs no marker at "20" | The quenched LHS-1 20 wt% Td point is not visible.
- p.22 vs p.23 Slides 22–23 | text contradicts text | "LESTR capable of testing under simulated Lunar conditions, can cycle ~40K to 125K" vs "Samples held at 12.0945 K and 7.37E-6 Torr for 24 h" | The hold temperature (12.0945 K) is below the stated ~40 K lower limit of the rig. Quoting it to six significant figures also suggests more precision than the measurement supports.
- p.23 Slide 23 | qualitative only | "No signs of catastrophic failure or significant thermal stress" | No measured property before or after LESTR is reported, only photographs, so there is no number to extract.
- p.13 Slide 13 | non-numeric value / unexplained mark | "T = 17.5 min, RT" and "T = 25 min, RT"; "Fully Crystallized*" | Room temperature is not given as a number (the p.14 chart plots it at about 22 °C). The asterisk on the neat-PHB "Fully Crystallized*" has no footnote. The cooling programme is not stated.
- p.19 Slide 19 | inconsistent simulant name | "10% and 20% LMS-1D" vs "Lunar Mare (LMS-1)" (p.6) and "20% LMS-1" elsewhere | Filament was made with LMS-1D, a different designation from the LMS-1 used for the molded samples, which is easy to confuse. No filament or printed-part property values are given.
- p.6 Slide 6 | missing unit / undefined statistic | "Particle Density" (no unit; values 1.3–1.7), "Particle Size (um)" | The density unit is missing and the values are implausibly low for grain density (probably bulk density). The size statistic is undefined, and it does not match the SRT d50 values on p.42–46 (e.g. LMS-1 ≈140 µm on the radar vs d50 ≈95 µm; MGS-1 ≈71 vs ≈40 µm). The size-axis origin is not a labelled value (about 32 µm), and LMS-1 sits at the axis maximum (140).
- p.41–46 Slides 41–46 | cited vendor data | "Source: Space Resource Technologies" | The particle size distributions are vendor fact sheets (doc IDs 003-08/003-01/003-02/001-12/003-05-001-1225), not NASA measurements. The LSP-2 sheet uses a different chart style (0–100 % in steps of 10, x to ~2000 µm).
- p.30 Slide 30 | missing metadata | "Average Lap Shear Strength (MPa)", "PHB SC", "PHB Anneal" | Welding parameters, joint geometry, n and error-bar type are not given. The abbreviations are explained only by the micrograph captions.
