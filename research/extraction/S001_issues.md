# S001 source issues: Li et al. 2020, Polymers 12:1724 (PLA + CLRS-1)

Page numbers are PDF page indices. General note: from p.4 onward the PDF text layer holds two overlapping copies of the text (the published layout plus an embedded "FOR PEER REVIEW" layer). `pdftotext -layout` output is interleaved and hard to read, but every table value can still be found on its page. Figure 9 (p.11) is a raster image, so its printed data labels are not in the text layer.

- p.5 Section 3.1 text vs p.6 Table 3 | text contradicts table | "10 wt.% of CLRS-1 into PLA only shows a 7.39% reduction in the bending strength" vs Table 3 "10 wt% CLRS-1 82.7 ± 3.3 −5.98 … 3.05 ± 0.15 7.39" | Table 3 gives the flexural-strength %R.D. for 10 wt% as −5.98. The figure 7.39 is the modulus %R.D., and it is an increase, not a reduction.
- p.6 Table 3 | rows don't add up (minor) | "86.6 ± 7.0 −1.55" and "82.7 ± 3.3 −5.98" vs neat "88.0 ± 3.0" | Computing from the printed means gives −1.59% and −6.02%. The printed %R.D. values were probably taken from unrounded means. The Table 2 and Table 3 modulus %R.D. values do match their means.
- p.10 Figure 8b vs Figure 8a / Table 5 | legend inconsistency | Fig. 8b legend: "neat PLA" (red), "5 wt% PLA/CLRS-1" (black) vs Fig. 8a legend: "neat PLA" (black) | The tallest and highest-temperature DTG peak (~360 °C) is black in 8b, which matches the neat-PLA Tmax of 363 °C in Table 5. The 8b legend appears to swap neat PLA and 5 wt%.
- p.11 Figure 9a vs p.10 Table 5 | figure label contradicts table | Fig. 9a neat PLA melting label "149.85" vs Table 5 Neat PLA Tm "149.98" | Same first-heating measurement, two different numbers. The CSV uses the table value. The other Fig. 9a labels (123.38, 114.92, 114.09, 148.49, 148.19) match Table 5.
- p.11 Figure 9c | undefined quantity | labels "55.52", "55.63", "56.34" on first-cooling curves | Neither the caption nor the text says what these temperatures are. They sit at the step change on cooling and were extracted as glass_transition (cooling), confidence medium. They differ from the heating Tg in Table 5 (60/61/62 °C).
- p.11 Figure 9b | label legibility | "126.73" | This low-resolution raster label could read 126.73 or 126.75. Taken as 126.73, confidence medium.
- p.2 Section 2.1 vs p.12 Table 6 | inconsistent numbers / ambiguous label | "PLA pellets (trade name 4032D) with an average molecular weight (Mw) of 2.49 × 105 g/mol" vs Table 6 "raw PLA 75.85" (kg/mol) | 2.49 × 10^5 g/mol = 249 kg/mol, but Table 6 gives "raw PLA" as 75.85 kg/mol. The paper never says whether "raw PLA" means pellets, filament, printed parts or something else.
- p.3 Section 2.3 vs p.11 Section 3.4 | text contradicts text | "The recycling process lasted for about 2 h." vs "The total process lasted for approximately 3 h." | The two sections give different recycling durations.
- p.12 Table 6 | values can't be tied to a specific composition | "recycled PLA 73.92 1.72" | The recycled PLA was recovered from printed PLA/CLRS-1 parts, but the CLRS-1 content (5 or 10 wt%) of those parts is not stated.
- p.2 Section 2.1 | typo / ambiguous label | "The median particle diameter of CFLRS-1 is about 80–110 µm" | "CFLRS-1" is probably a typo for CLRS-1. This is the size before ball milling. After milling the text gives an average of "~2.5 µm" (p.6), not a median, so d50_um is left empty.
- p.3 Sections 2.2/2.4 | standard designation typo | "ISO 527:2:2012" | The correct designation is ISO 527-2:2012. The CSV uses ISO 527-2:2012.
- p.3 Section 2.4 | specimen count not exact | "At least five specimens of each sample were tested" | No exact n is given, so the n column is empty (noted as n>=5).
- p.10 Table 5 | interpretation | "RCa (wt.%) … 0.78 / 6.57 / 11.95" | The residual char at 700 °C is not presented as the measured filler content (5 wt% gives 6.57). regolith_wt_actual is left empty and RC is recorded as its own property, residual_char.
