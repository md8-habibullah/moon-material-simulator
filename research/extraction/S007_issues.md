# S007 source issues: Moazen et al. 2025, Acta Astronautica 237:409–420 (LDPE + LMS-1D)

- p.10 Fig. 10b vs Table 3 | figure label differs from table | "8.73" (Fig. 10b, TS1-20 bar) vs "8.74 ± 0.55" (Table 3, TS1-20) | Same quantity (tensile strength at break), the chart label and table disagree by 0.01 MPa.
- p.10 Fig. 10b vs Table 3 | figure label differs from table | "7.47" (Fig. 10b, TS1-30 bar) vs "7.48 ± 0.40" (Table 3 and Table 4, TS1-30) | Same quantity, off by 0.01 MPa.
- p.10 Fig. 10d vs Table 3 | figure label differs from table | "0.86" (Fig. 10d, SS2-10) vs "0.85 ± 0.09" (Table 3) | 0.2% offset yield strength, off by 0.01 MPa.
- p.10 Fig. 10d vs Table 3 | figure label differs from table | "0.99" (Fig. 10d, SS2-20) vs "0.98 ± 0.23" (Table 3) | 0.2% offset yield strength, off by 0.01 MPa. (Fig. 10d TS1-30 is labelled "1" vs table "0.99 ± 0.16".)
- p.9 §3.3 vs p.10 Table 3 / Fig. 10c | text percentages not reproducible from the table | "adding regolith increased stiffness by 13.8, 38, and 52 % at 10, 20, and 30 wt%" vs "121.7 ± 6.2" (LDPE), "164.9 ± 4.4" (SS2-20), "180.8 ± 5.6" (SS2-30) | Table 3 gives +13.8% (SS2-10), +35.5% (SS2-20), +48.6% (SS2-30), +38.9% (TS1-20), +53.0% (TS1-30). Fig. 10c draws the "38%" and "52%" arrows on the SS2-20 and SS2-30 bars, where they do not match; they are closer to the TS1 values. The 10 wt% arrow is labelled "13%", while the text says 13.8.
- p.9 §3.3 vs p.10 Table 3 | text contradicts table | "In the presence of regolith, yield strength values remained generally similar to pure LDPE or slightly lower" vs "1.31 ± 0.15" (SS2-30) and "1.16 ± 0.20" (LDPE) | SS2-30 yield strength is higher than neat LDPE. The text's next sentence ("no clear or consistent trend") is consistent with the table.
- p.3 §2.2 vs p.5 §3.1 | inconsistent supplementary-table reference | "reported as percentages in Table S4 (see Supporting Document)" vs "summarized in Table. S2 (Supporting Document)" | The same diameter-percentage data is referred to as Table S4 and Table S2, and p.2 already uses "Tables S1 and S2" for the regolith composition and particle size. (The supplement was not available for checking.)
- p.10 Table 3 | composition and route of the neat baseline not labelled | "LDPE" | The neat row does not say which grade or route. From the text it is d-LDPE (DOW 722) extruded on the 3Devo single-screw extruder; the number of passes (SS1 or SS2) is not stated.
- p.8 Fig. 8 | values cannot be tied to one filament route | "Warpage and arc radius analysis of 3D printed rectangular cuboids" | The source does not say whether the 10/20/30 wt% cuboids used SS2 or TS1 filament. The x-axis is labelled "Regolith Content (%)" with no wt/vol. The warpage numbers exist only in the chart; the text gives only "47 %". Error bars are not defined (SD?) and n is not given.
- p.7 §3.2 | values cannot be tied to one filament route | "composite Benchy exhibits a smaller mean. Deviation of − 0.193 mm" | The 30 wt% Benchy filament route (SS2 or TS1) is not stated.
- p.10 Table 3 | uncertainty type not defined | "1.16 ± 0.20" | "±" is not defined as SD, SE or CI (5 specimens per material, p.4).
- p.10 Fig. 10a | elongation not reported numerically | "Representative stress–strain curves for each material are provided in Fig. 10a" | Elongation/strain at break exists only as one representative curve per material (the one closest to the average), so no mean or SD can be taken from the paper.
- p.11 Table 4 | typos | "MDPE + Martial regolith" and "12 Mm/s" | "Martial" should be Martian (ref [16] uses a Martian simulant) and "Mm/s" should be mm/s.
- p.11 Table 4 | values cannot be tied to a specific composition | "Max regolith content" column with "Tensile strength: 67.0 MPa", "53.0 ± 2.8 MPa", "Flexural strength: 132.21 MPa", etc. | Cited property values are listed beside each study's maximum regolith content, but the table does not say at which composition each property was measured (e.g. PLA [15] made 5 and 10 wt%, MDPE [16] made 5 and 10 wt%). The DIW row gives "74 vol%" while the others use wt%.
- p.11 Table 4 vs p.2 §1 | possible inconsistency in cited PEEK data | "Tensile strength: 67.0 MPa" ([17], max 30 wt%) vs "reductions of 26.8 % in tensile strength" ([17], 30 wt%) | Not a contradiction in itself, but the neat PEEK baseline behind the 26.8% is not given, so the two cannot be cross-checked.
- p.4 §2.6 | test standard text uses a non-Latin character | "ASTM D638-type ІV" | The text layer uses a Cyrillic "І" in "ІV", which may break text search for "IV".
