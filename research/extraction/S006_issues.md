# S006 source issues

Source: Christy, Ranaiefar, Fuchs (NASA GRC), "Polymer/Regolith Composites for In-Space Manufacturing on the Moon and Mars", WAMS 2026 slides (NTRS 20260003641). All charts are raster images, so none of the chart numbers are in the PDF text layer.

- p.20 Slide 20 (repeated p.26, p.27) | contradicts companion deck | "17.96 / 19.62 / 20.05 / 16.45 / 23.19" error bars ±2.88 / ±3.20 / ±1.74 / ±4.32 / ±4.15 MPa (JEZ-1/LHS-1/LMS-1/LSP-2/MGS-1) vs S011 slide 17, same means, error bars ±3.21 / ±1.75 / ±4.30 / ±4.14 / ±2.42 | The means match the ACS deck exactly, but the error bars do not. Each S006 error bar equals the S011 error bar of the sample before it (JEZ-1 gets the neat-PHB value ±2.89), which looks like an off-by-one shift in the error column. Treat the S006 std values as suspect.
- p.20 Slide 20 | missing metadata | "Average Tensile Strength (MPa)" | Number of specimens, error-bar type (SD or SE?), test standard and test temperature are not given.
- p.16 Slide 16 (repeated p.17, p.18) | missing data point | "LMS-1" legend entry vs no LMS-1 marker at "80" on the Crystallinity chart | No LMS-1 80 wt% crystallinity marker is visible, and it cannot be hidden behind the other markers. It is also missing on the slow-cooled backup chart (p.36).
- p.16 Slide 16 | missing data point | "LHS-1" legend entry vs no LHS-1 marker at "80" on the Decomposition Temperature chart | LHS-1 80 wt% Td is not plotted here or on the slow-cooled backup chart (p.36). The ACS deck (S011 slide 9) does show it, at about 276 °C.
- p.16 Slide 16 | ambiguous sample condition | "Crystallinity" / "Decomposition Temperature" (no cooling condition stated) vs p.36 "Slow Cooled Samples" | The main-section charts don't say how the films were cooled. Only the backup slide shows that these are the slow-cooled data, and its points match point for point.
- p.15 Slide 15 | formula / normalisation unclear | "X_C = ∆H_m / ∆H_100% × 100" vs 80 wt% values of ~26–30 % (p.16) | The formula has no (1 − w_filler) term. Without one, a film with 20 wt% polymer could not exceed 20 % crystallinity, so the plotted values must have been normalised to polymer content without saying so. The ∆H_100% value used is not given either.
- p.16 vs p.26 | possible inconsistency / ambiguous method | 40 wt% Td on p.16, e.g. LSP-2 ≈282.4 °C, LHS-1 ≈281.9 °C, MGS-1 ≈269.0 °C vs p.26 "279.5", "280.2", "266.2" | For LSP-2, LHS-1 and MGS-1 the values differ by 1.7–2.9 °C for the same filler and loading (JEZ-1 and LMS-1 agree within 0.4 °C). p.26 is injection molded and p.16 is compression-molded film, but p.26 does not say how Td was measured (DSC endotherm peak or TGA).
- p.36 Slide 36 (Quenched Samples, Decomposition Temperature) | missing data point | "Quenched LHS-1" legend entry vs no marker at "20" | The quenched LHS-1 20 wt% Td point is not visible and cannot be hidden by the other markers.
- p.36 Slide 36 | missing metadata | "Quenched Samples" / "Slow Cooled Samples" | Quench medium, cooling rates and the DSC heating rate are not given.
- p.6 Slide 6 | missing unit / implausible value | "Particle Density" axis 1.1–1.7 (no unit) | No unit is printed. Values of 1.3–1.7 are far below mineral grain density (~2.7–3.2 g/cm³), so this is probably bulk density, but the slide doesn't say.
- p.6 Slide 6 | undefined statistic / axis | "Particle Size (um)" axis labels "40 … 140" | The slide doesn't say which statistic this is (mean, median, mode). The axis origin is not a labelled value (about 32 µm, extrapolated from the label spacing). LMS-1 sits exactly at the axis maximum (140), so its value may be clipped. Where the particle data came from is not stated.
- p.28 Slide 28 | missing metadata | "Average Lap Shear Strength (MPa)", "PHB SC", "PHB Anneal" | Abbreviations are explained only by the micrograph captions ("PHB Slow Cooled", "PHB Quench + Annealed 1h"). Welding parameters, joint geometry, n and error-bar type are not given.
- p.13–15 Slides 13–15 | missing metadata | "Heat Flow (mW)" DSC curves | Heating rate, sample mass and atmosphere are not given. Td is taken as the peak of a DSC endotherm, not from TGA.
