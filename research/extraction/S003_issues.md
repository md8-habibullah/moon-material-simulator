# S003 source issues

Gelino et al., "Selection, Production, and Properties of Regolith Polymer Composites for Lunar Construction" (NTRS 20230015024). Page numbers are PDF page indices, which match the printed page numbers in this file. Chart values quoted below were digitized from 150 dpi renders (about ±0.1 GPa or ±0.5 MPa) and are given only as evidence. They are not in S003.csv.

## Text contradicts its own table or figure

- p.11 Table 4 / Strength Properties Discussion | swapped values | "lower flexural strength in the 0º and 90º orientations at 16.55 and 23.86 MPa respectfully" vs Table 4 "Flexural Strength (MPa) 24 3 16.6 0.8" | The text swaps the orientations. The table (0º = 24, 90º = 16.6) agrees with Figure 14 and with p.18 ("flexural strength of 24 MPa in the 0° orientation"). The table values are used.
- p.11 Strength Properties Discussion | different numbers | "highest recorded in the 0º orientation at 49.22 MPa" vs Table 4 "Compression Strength (MPa) 50 2" | 49.22 rounds to 49, not 50. The Figure 14 bar (~49) matches the text. The text's 46.33 (90º) does match the table's 46.3.
- p.9 Ultimate Flexural Strength paragraph | different numbers | "80:20 BP-1: PLA demonstrated the highest ultimate flexural strength value for the test samples that were cut perpendicular to the grain (17.8 MPa)" vs Table 3 "80/20 BP1/PLA 90º 17.70" and "80/20 BP1/PLA w/ Additive 90º 18.20" | The value differs from the table (17.8 vs 17.70), and in Table 3 the additive formulation, not plain 80:20 BP-1, has the highest 90º value.
- p.9 Ultimate Flexural Strength paragraph | different numbers | "80:20 LHS-1: PLA had the highest ... parallel to the grain (33.4 MPa); however, 80:20 BP1:PLA was 2nd only by 1.03 MPa or roughly 3%" vs Table 3 "80/20 LHS-1/PLA 0º 34.40", "80/20 BP1/PLA 0º 32.00", "w/ Additive 0º 32.03" | Table 3 gives 34.40, not 33.4. The table gap is 2.37 to 2.40 MPa (~7%), not 1.03 MPa (~3%).
- p.7-8 vs p.10 Table 3 | missing data | "SpaceFactory performed compression and tensile testing on all the formulations" vs Table 3 "80/20 LHS-1/PLA ... - - -" | Table 3 has no compressive or tensile values for LHS-1. Figure 13 still draws tensile bars for LHS-1 (see the Figure 13 entry).
- p.13 Density Characterization vs p.14 Table 6 | text contradicts table | "The data reveals that the density of the formulations was the same or reduced following printing. This is expected because ... the polymer goes through additional consolidation" vs Table 6 pellet Disp. "1.56*" to ambient "1.82" / TVAC "1.79" (80:20 BP-1), "1.73" to "1.78" (additive), "1.80" to "1.82" (LHS-1) | In the table, densities stayed the same or increased after printing, which is also what "consolidation" would cause. "Reduced" is probably a slip, perhaps for porosity.
- p.11 Figure 12 vs p.10 Table 3 | figure contradicts table | Figure 12 bars for "80:20 BP1:PLA" compressive modulus 90º ~7.75 and 0º ~4.04 vs Table 3 "80/20 BP1/PLA 8.95 / 4.43" | The BP-1 categories in Figure 12 seem mis-assigned. The "80:20 BP1:PLA" bars match the Table 3 "w/ Additive" values (7.73 / 4.04). The "70:30 BP-1:PLA" bars (~8.96 / ~4.42) match Table 3 80/20 (8.95 / 4.43). The "w/ Additive" 90º bar (~5.37) matches 70/30 (5.38). Several flexural-modulus bars match no table cell: 85:15 0º ~6.5 vs 5.71, 85:15 90º ~2.2 vs 2.63, additive 0º ~6.1 vs 4.68, 70:30 0º ~4.25 vs 3.22. Only LHS-1 and 85:15 compressive agree. The table is used. Figure 12 was not digitized.
- p.12 Figure 13 vs p.10 Table 3 | figure contradicts table | Figure 13 "80:20 BP1:PLA w/ Additive" tensile bars ~11.3 (90º) and ~5.2 (0º) vs Table 3 "15.36" / "11.70" | The tensile series looks shifted by one category. The additive bars match 85/15 (11.40 / 5.18). "85:15 BP1:PLA" has no tensile bars, although Table 3 has values. "80:20 LHS-1:PLA" has tensile bars (~14.1 / ~11.0) although Table 3 prints "-"; these match 70/30 BP-1 (14.19 / 10.92). Other mismatches: 85:15 flexural 90º ~7.1 vs 12.53, 70:30 flexural 90º ~12.9 vs 17.50, additive flexural 0º ~28.1 vs 32.03 and 90º ~15.1 vs 18.20, additive compressive 90º ~38.9 vs 46.80. For this reason the LHS-1 SpaceFactory tensile bars were NOT extracted.
- p.14 Figure 17 vs Table 6 | figure contradicts table | Figure 17 Ambient Print bar ~1.26 g/cc for "70:30 BP-1:PLA" vs Table 6 "70:30 BP-1: PLA ... -" (no ambient print) | The bar matches Pure PLA's ambient density (1.26), which is not otherwise plotted. It looks mis-plotted and was not extracted.

## Wrong or inconsistent units

- p.11 Strength Properties Discussion | unit MPa vs GPa | "in the 90º orientation it remained essentially the same at 3.65 MPa" vs Table 4 "Flexural Modulus (GPa) ... 3.7" | Should be GPa.
- p.11 Strength Properties Discussion | unit MPa vs GPa | "Compressive modulus was comparable in both 0º and 90º at 7.45 and 6.41 MPa" vs Table 4 "Compression Modulus (GPa) 7.5 ... 6.41" | Should be GPa.
- p.11 Strength Properties Discussion | unit GPa vs MPa | "Typical flexural strength of unreinforced concrete is similar and are in the range of 3-5 GPa" vs "a flexural strength of 4-8 MPa" (lumber, same paragraph) | The concrete flexural strength should be about 3-5 MPa.
- p.18 Conclusions | unit GPa vs MPa | "Typical flexural strength of unreinforced concrete is similar and are in the range of 3-5 GPa" | Same error repeated.
- p.6 Beta Materials Chemistry | possible unit error | "Approximately 10 µg was used for each sample" | TGA samples are normally about 10 mg, so this may be a µg/mg slip (low confidence).
- p.4 vs p.5 Figure 6 | magnitude | "The pressure increases to ~0.8 torr" vs "the pressure rises in the chamber to 0.080 - 0.100 torr" (same paragraph) and the Figure 6 axis maximum "1.00E-01" | ~0.8 torr should be ~0.08 torr.

## Table rows or columns that don't add up, duplicated or ambiguous labels

- p.6 Table 1 | wrong label | "Tensile Properties ASTM D5379" | ASTM D5379 is the V-notched beam in-plane shear method. Table 4 reports in-plane shear, but Table 1 lists "Tensile Properties" twice and has no shear row.
- p.9 Table 2 | duplicated labels | "BP-1: PLA pellet" (3 rows) and "BP-1: PLA TVAC" (3 rows) | These rows can only be told apart by the Target column (70:30 / 80:20 / 85:15).
- p.9 Table 2 | possible arithmetic mismatch | "BP-1: PLA TVAC 80:20 - 71:29 11.9" | (80-71)/80 = 11.25%; 11.9% implies ~70.5 wt.%, which prints as 70, not 71. The additive rows show the same problem: TVAC "71:29*" with "9.7" implies ~72.2; pellet "72:28*"/"71:29*" (avg 71.5) with "8.7" implies ~73.0. The deviation formula is not stated, and the other rows agree within rounding.
- p.9 Table 2 | ambiguous footnote | "* CP-L01 is consumed during the compounding process." | It is unclear whether the starred ratios are BP-1:PLA after CP-L01 is lost or are measured on a different basis.
- p.14 Table 7 | value built on a flagged input | "80:20 BP-1: PLA 24.1" (Pellet Porosity) vs Table 6 "1.56*" ("Assumed measurement error") | The 24.1% pellet porosity fits the flagged displacement density (eqs 1.1-1.3 give ~24% with TGA 70 wt.%), so it is probably unreliable too.
- p.14 Table 7 vs Table 6 | possible inconsistency | "80:20 LHS-1: PLA 13.8 15.1" (pellet vs ambient porosity) vs Table 6 Disp. "1.80" (pellet) and "1.82" (ambient) | The ambient print is denser than the pellet at nearly the same filler content (Table 2: 75 vs 74-75 wt.%), yet its porosity is higher. Eqs 1.1-1.3 cannot reproduce this (they give ~12.7% for 1.82 at 75 wt.%).
- p.13 vs p.14 Table 6 | flag inconsistent with text | "some of the pycnometer values are assumed to be in error" vs Table 6 "1.56*" under "Disp." | One flagged value is a displacement value, not a pycnometer value.
- p.13 Table 5 / p.14 Table 6 | source-flagged values | "Pure BP-1 3.87*", "2.31*", "2.53*", "3.2*" | The source flags these as "Assumed measurement error". They are kept in the CSV with that flag in the notes.
- p.14 Table 7 vs p.13 text | method | "Collected displacement data of pellets and printed parts was used along with mass fractions from TGA to calculate porosity" vs Table 2 TGA "-" for every TVAC/Ambient row | Printed parts have only ash values, so the print porosities cannot have used TGA fractions as described.
- p.20 Table 8 | implausible value | "PI (Aurum PL450C) 55 238 388 -273" (Tg column, °C) | A Tg of -273 °C is absolute zero and physically impossible, probably a placeholder or error. The value is kept with a warning in the notes.
- p.20 Table 8 | layout / clipped text | "184." / "5" (PI 77K σUT); "PEEK (Victrex" (no closing parenthesis) | 184.5 is printed across two lines. Row labels are clipped or overprinted in the rendered table ("FEP (Teflon 100X)", "(Generic)" hidden for PHA/PHB and HDPE), so the CSV uses the text-layer labels.

## Values that can't be tied to a specific composition or condition

- p.9 Table 2 vs Tables 3, 4, 6, 7 | missing row | Table 2 has no "LHS-1: PLA TVAC" row | The achieved filler wt.% of the TVAC-printed LHS-1 specimens (the selected Beta material) is not reported, so regolith_wt_actual is empty for those rows.
- p.11 Table 4 / p.6 | unstated condition | "The formulation selected as the final Beta material was sent to an independent test laboratory" | The paper does not say whether the independent-lab specimens were printed in TVAC or ambient. It states only that testing was done at "prevailing laboratory conditions".
- p.5 vs p.6 | sample count | "Four samples of each material were tested in each of layer orientations per test" vs "Three to four samples of each formulation in each orientation were tested" | n for the SpaceFactory flexural and compression results is ambiguous (n is left empty; tensile n = 4).
- p.8 vs Beta sections | missing grade | "Polylactic Acid (PLA) (Luminy LX175) was selected as the binder in a second version of the Alpha materials" | The PLA grade used for the Beta materials (Tables 2-7) is never stated.
- p.1/p.2 vs p.4 | process terminology | "A Fused Filament Fabrication (FFF) construction process" vs "outfitted with an additive construction screw style pellet extruder" | Printing is pellet-fed (the companion slides S012 call it Fused Granular Fabrication), so the CSV codes it as pellet_AM.
- p.4 vs p.8 | inconsistent conditions | "Vacuum was maintained at 0.250 torr" (Alpha) vs "Using PLA in vacuum printing at ~10-3 torr significantly mitigated porosity" and "Additive construction tests at 0.30 to 0.20 torr" | The Alpha vacuum level is reported differently in different places. This does not affect any extracted value.
