# S005 source issues (Azami, Aubin-Fournier, Skonieczny, preprint 202606.1562.v1, FE modelling of AM regolith-filled PEEK)

Page numbers are PDF page indices (PDF p.1 is the Preprints.org cover, so PDF page = printed "N of 23" + 1).

- p.11 Table 7 vs p.13 Table 8 and p.16 Table 9 | different numbers for the identical run | Table 7 "15 0.3×0.3 31.94 1 394612 10 6.45 1.29 1.96" vs Table 8 "15 0.30×0.30 31.94 1 394,612 10 6.45 1.29 1.97" (Table 9 CAD 15 also "1.97") | Same CAD file, RVE, mesh size and element count, but Ecomposite is 1.96 GPa in the mesh study and 1.97 GPa elsewhere.
- p.12 Section 6.2 text vs p.12 Figure 2 caption / p.13 Table 8 | wrong label | "Figure 2a shows the longitudinal strain field E11 for the 0.15 × 0.15 mm RVE (CAD file No. 31)" vs "(a) CAD file No. 0 with an RVE size of 0.15 mm × 0.15 mm" | The 0.15 mm RVE is CAD 0 in the caption and in Table 8. "No. 31" looks like a typo.
- p.3 Section 1 (values cited from [6]) vs p.16 Table 10 and p.10 Section 5 | numbers don't reconcile | "improvements ranging from 6–41% for composites containing 10–40 wt% filler ... at 50 wt%, where the modulus was only 16% higher relative to pure PEEK ... the 30 wt% composite demonstrated ... about 19.3% relative to pure PEEK" vs Table 10 EExperiment "1.23", "1.40", "1.55", "1.27" and "Elastic modulus of PEEK (Em ): 1.14 GPa" | These % gains imply a different neat-PEEK modulus at each loading: 1.16 (10 wt%), 1.17 (30 wt%), 1.10 (40 wt%) and 1.09 GPa (50 wt%). With Em = 1.14 GPa the gains would be +7.9%, +22.8%, +36% and +11.4%. Either the Table 10 experimental moduli or the cited % values come from different datasets or specimens.
- p.7 Section 3.5 vs p.6 Eq. 6 / p.7 Table 3 | doesn't add up | "the back-calculated (bulk) density of the regolith phase is 2.77 g/cm3" vs "ρdense = ... = 1.6469 g/cm3" | The inverse rule of mixtures with 40 wt% regolith, PEEK 1.30 g/cm3 and ρc = 1.6469 gives a regolith-phase density of about 2.75 g/cm3, not 2.77. A value of 2.77 corresponds to ρc = 1.650, which is the Table 3 value calculated from 2.77 itself (circular). This shifts the 12.1% regolith porosity slightly (about 12.8% with 2.75).
- p.8 Eq. 12 vs p.6 Tables 1-2 | wrong unit / small arithmetic mismatch | "∑(Wi /ρi ) = 31.191 cm3 /g" | With Wi in percent from Table 1 and ρi from Table 2 the sum is 31.128 (%·cm3/g). The printed unit is off by a factor of 100 (it should be 0.311 cm3/g with Wi as fractions). Table 5 fractions match 31.191, but the Voigt/Reuss results (143.94 / 89.40 GPa, p.9) reproduce exactly only with the 31.128 normalisation. The effect is negligible.
- p.6 Table 1 | composition doesn't add up | "Weight Fraction Wi (%)" column sums to 98.1 | The oxide wt% sum to 98.1%, not 100%. Minor oxides and LOI are omitted without comment. The 3.15 g/cm3 density is consistent with renormalising to 98.1.
- p.6 Table 1 caption and p.7/p.9 text vs p.23 reference list | wrong citation | "LMS-1D simulant [14]" and "comprising approximately 11% of the total regolith volume [14]" vs "14. Patnaik, P. Handbook of inorganic chemicals" | The LMS-1D fact sheet (composition and <1 µm fines fraction) is cited as [14], which is an inorganic-chemicals handbook. The real source of the 11% fines figure cannot be traced.
- p.6 Section 3.4 | ambiguous composition label | "A backscattered electron (BSE) SEM image of the 40 wt% PEEK–regolith composite filament" vs next sentence "The measured density of the 40 wt% regolith composite is 1.623 g/cm3" | "40 wt% PEEK–regolith" could be read as 40 wt% PEEK. Interpreted as 40 wt% regolith from the next sentence and Section 3.5.
- p.7 Section 3.5 and p.10 Section 5 | values not tied to a source | "Assuming PEEK has a density of 1.30 g/cm3" and "Elastic modulus of PEEK (Em ): 1.14 GPa" | Neat-PEEK inputs that drive every FE result are given with no reference, grade or test method.
- p.16 Table 10 / p.3 Section 1 | process not stated | "experimental modulus from AM coupons" | S005 never says which AM process was used for the [6] coupons. FFF is only implied ("composite filament" p.6, "during FFF" p.22), so the experimental rows record process=FFF as an assumption.
- p.16 Table 10 | not reproducible from printed numbers | "10 4.96 1.27 ± 0.01 1.23 3.50" | (1.27 − 1.23)/1.23 = 3.25%, not 3.50%. The Diff (%) column was evidently computed from unrounded FE means (for example 1.2733 gives 3.52%). The other rows show the same small offsets (5.88 vs 6.11, 10.13 vs 10.32, 55.35 vs 55.12).
- p.16 Table 9 | numbering gap | CAD files "13", "15", "16" for 50 wt% | CAD 14 is missing with no explanation (it is possibly a discarded RVE). Minor.
- p.19 Section 6.4.1 vs p.16 Table 10 | minor inconsistency | "decreases from ∼ 1.96 GPa (defect-free) to ∼ 1.45 GPa" vs "50 31.94 1.97 ± 0.02" | The defect-free reference for the single-layer case is quoted as 1.96 GPa, while p.21 and Section 7 use 1.97 GPa. The single-layer RVE (0.3 × 0.24 mm) is a different domain, so this may be its own baseline, but this is not stated.

## Cross-checks that passed (no issue)
- Abstract "1.27 GPa at 10 wt% to 1.97 GPa at 50 wt%", "within 3.5–10.1%" and "experimental modulus decreases to 1.27 GPa" match Table 10.
- The Table 10 means and standard deviations reproduce from the three Table 9 RVEs per composition.
- The Table 6 volume fractions and Voigt/Reuss/average matrix moduli reproduce from Eqs. 14-20 (ρreg 2.77, ρPEEK 1.30, Em 1.14, Er 6.45). Table 3 densities reproduce from 2.77/1.30.
- Eq. 13: 116.67 × (1 − 3.85 × 0.121)^4.62 = 6.44 GPa (printed ≈ 6.45). Tiling Voigt/Reuss/Hill 1.911/1.903/1.907 reproduce from the nine tile moduli.
