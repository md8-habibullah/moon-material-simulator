# S002 source issues: Alexander-Roy et al. 2025, Polymers 17:2685 (BMD-PLA vs PLA)

Page numbers are PDF page indices.

## Abstract cross-check (no mismatch)
- p.1 Abstract vs p.7 Tables 4/5 | precision only | "maximum compressive yield strength for the BMD-reinforced samples is 27.68 MPa with … 100% infill, one shell, and 90◦" vs Table 5 batch 6 (100/1/90) "27.7 ± 0.4" | Consistent after rounding. Batch 6 is also the BMD-PLA maximum σy in Table 5.
- p.1 Abstract vs p.7 Table 4 | precision only | "maximum compressive yield strength for the PLA samples is 63.05 MPa with … 100% infill, three shells, and 0◦" vs Table 4 batch 7 (100/3/0) "63.1 ± 1.6" | Consistent after rounding. Batch 7 is the PLA maximum (batch 5 is 62.2).
- p.1 Abstract | consistent | "The composite samples exhibit an increase in strength when layer lines are aligned with loading axis, whereas the PLA samples decreased" | Tables 4/5 agree. Every BMD-PLA 90° batch has a higher σy than its 0° pair, and every PLA 90° batch has a lower one.

## Issues
- p.9 Section 3 text vs p.7 Tables 4/5 (and p.8 text) | text contradicts table | "The elastic modulus of BMD-PLA … was consistently more rigid than PLA for the same print settings" vs Table 4 batch 5 "2.61 ± 0.12" and Table 5 batch 5 "2.41 ± 0.18" | In 7 of the 8 batches the BMD-PLA E is lower than the PLA E. p.8 itself says the moduli are "slightly reduced from the PLA to BMD-PLA".
- p.11 Section 3 text vs p.7 Tables 4/5 | text contradicts table | "Across all print parameters except batch 2, BMD-PLA displayed higher elastic modulus values than PLA." vs Table 5 batch 2 "1.15 ± 0.10" and Table 4 batch 2 "1.03 ± 0.04" | The tables show the reverse: batch 2 is the only batch where the BMD-PLA modulus is higher than the PLA modulus.
- p.11 Section 3 text | wrong table reference | "all models fall outside the range seen in Table 4" | Table 4 holds PLA data. The model predictions are for BMD-PLA, so the comparison should be with Table 5.
- p.11 Table 8 vs p.10 Eq. (3) | table values inconsistent with the stated method | Eq. (3) "Eeff = EmEd / (f Em + (1 − f) Ed)" with "f is the volume fraction of the dispersion" and "30.9%" vs Table 8 Reuss "7.81 / 7.32 / 4.89" | With Em 2.54, Ed 108.9/46.2/8.4 GPa and f = 0.309, Eq. 3 gives about 3.64/3.59/3.24 GPa. The printed 7.81/7.32/4.89 are reproduced only if f and (1−f) are swapped, which is a 69.1% dispersion. This is a checker calculation, logged here only; the CSV keeps the printed values. The Luo values (5.51/5.10/3.65) could not be reproduced from Eq. (4) as extracted from the text layer, so they are unverified.
- p.10–11 Section 3 and Table 8 vs bibliography (p.20–21) | broken citations | "Reuss model [36]", "Work by Luo … [37]", "2.54 GPa [35]", "108.9 [40] 46.2 [41] 8.4 [39]" vs reference list "[35] ASTM D695-23", "[36] ASTM D638-14", "[37] Grafarend…", "[39] Reuss…", "[40] Luo…", "[41] Farah… PLA" | In-text numbers in this section seem shifted against the bibliography. The cited lunar-simulant moduli point to Reuss 1929, Luo 2022 and a PLA review, none of which is a simulant-modulus source, so the CITED values in Table 8 cannot be traced.
- p.8 Section 3 text vs Figure 4 caption | text contradicts caption/Table 2 | "50% infill, shell, with 90◦ orientation (Figure 4a)" vs caption "(a) Compression batch 1" and Table 2 "1 50 1 0" | Batch 1 is 50% infill, 1 shell, 0°. The 50%/1-shell/90° condition is batch 2.
- p.11 Section 3 text vs p.13 Figure 9 | ordering contradicts figure | "A graph of PLA and BMD tensile yield strengths is given in Figure 9, organized from 0◦ infill to 90◦ infill." vs Figure 9 x-axis order "90° (9, 8, 4, 3) | 45° (5) | 0° (7, 6, 2, 1)" | The figure runs from 90° to 0°, the reverse of the text.
- p.13 Figure 9 | values only in chart; quality caveat | "Comparison of tensile yield strength for BMD-PLA infill." | Tensile yield strengths appear only as bars, with no table, n or error-bar definition. Only yield is shown, not tensile modulus or ultimate strength. p.11 says "the quality of the tensile response values … are called into question". The values were digitized (confidence medium), and the number of valid specimens per batch is unknown.
- p.3 Table 1 | values can't be tied to a single composition | "Basalt 46.2–47.74 … Ilmenite 8.64–8.93 … Anorthosite 5.16–5.33 … Binding Additive - … PLA <40.00" | Only manufacturer ranges are given. No single filler wt% is stated, and the binding additive is unquantified, so regolith_wt_target is left empty for all BMD-PLA rows.
- p.7 Figure 3 | values can't be tied to a specific composition | "Example compression stress–strain curve of one sample with 100% infill, three shells, 0◦ orientation." with in-plot "Young's Modulus: 2539.03 MPa Yield Strength: 62.77 MPa" | The material (PLA or BMD-PLA) is not stated. The values fit PLA batch 7, but this is a single specimen, so it is not extracted.
- p.8 Section 3 / p.9 Table 7 | ambiguous scope | "cross-sectional area measurements of the 50% infill samples were taken and used to adjust the stress values for compression batches 1–4" with Table 7 "Batch 1 (0◦ , 1 shell) 74.8 … Batch 4 (90◦ , 3 shell) 80.1" | The paper does not say whether these area fractions were measured on BMD-PLA, PLA or both, or whether they were applied to both materials' batches 1–4. They are kept in the row notes, not as data rows.
- p.5 Section 2.1 | specimen count ambiguity | "An additional specimen from each batch was prepared for digital image correlation (DIC)" vs "8 batches with 5 specimens each were printed for testing, for a total of 40 specimens per material" | It is unclear whether the DIC specimen is one of the 5 per batch. Fig. A1 says "Five samples were tested from each batch", so n = 5 was used, and n = 4 for BMD-PLA batch 7 after the outlier exclusion on p.6 ("sample 35, which was excluded").
- p.6–8 Tables 4 | missing values | Table 4 σult column "-" | No PLA compressive ultimate strength is reported because the "loading would continue to increase" (p.6). No rows were created for it.
- p.9 Table 6 | ambiguous labels | "BMD5-0 150-0001.jpg", "BMD5-90 150-0001.jpg", "BMD4-0 500-Shell0001.jpg" | The image filenames are the only link to a specimen, and the meaning of the "-0"/"-90" and "150"/"500" parts is not defined. Print conditions are left empty for these rows.
- p.13 Conclusions vs p.10–11 | method naming inconsistency | "The observed elastic modulus of the BMD-PLA is lower than that predicted by the rule of mixtures." vs Section 3, which uses the Reuss and Luo iso-stress models | The conclusion names a different model (rule of mixtures/Voigt) from the one actually used.
