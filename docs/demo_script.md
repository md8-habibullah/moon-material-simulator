# Demo script

Video: https://youtu.be/1Gtmq5XUytc

A walkthrough of the app for a live demo (about 90 seconds).

1. **Start neutral.** PLA with 30 wt% lunar regolith at room temperature. Point out the four
   predicted properties and the "vs pure polymer" deltas: the regolith makes PLA much stiffer and
   somewhat stronger in compression, at a small cost in tensile strength.
2. **Go to lunar noon.** Click the *Lunar noon* preset. PLA softens well below 127 °C, so the warning
   appears.
3. **Switch the binder to PEEK.** The warning clears: PEEK keeps its shape at lunar noon. Save both
   mixes with *Add to comparison*.
4. **Try Mars.** Switch the regolith to Martian. The grains in the 3D view turn rust-coloured, and the
   weaker grain–polymer bond shows up as lower strength. Save this mix too.
5. **Compare.** Scroll to *Comparison mode*: the chart shows which mix wins each property, and the
   table gives the exact values.
6. **Close on honesty.** Today's model is trained on a physics-informed synthetic dataset. Our next
   step is calibrating it against measured coupon data.
